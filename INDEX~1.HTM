The gap

A stroke is more likely to be missed if the patient is already in the hospital.

The paradox: in-hospital strokes are routinely identified later than community-onset events, despite occurring in a fully monitored environment. By the time symptoms appear, the reperfusion window is often already closed.

How it works

A wearable ultrasound patch.
AI signal processing on top.

Continuous, non-invasive, 24/7 monitoring of brain perfusion. No operator repositioning. No catheters. No probes. Built on four elements working together.

01

The ultrasound patch

Single-use wearable neck sensor. No clinician required after placement. Designed for continuous hospital use.

02

Bilateral symmetry check

Filters systemic noise versus localized occlusion by continuously comparing left and right carotid signals.

03

Personalized AI baseline

Learns the patient's unique hemodynamic signature and ignores natural fluctuations, keeping false positives low.

04

Wave intensity analysis

Measures the energy signature of a developing arterial blockage in real time, the cause of ischemia rather than its downstream effects.

278%
Signal spike at the moment of occlusion in our first animal study. Repeated across the protocol.
